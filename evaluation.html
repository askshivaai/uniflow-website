How an evaluation runs

Four steps. Nothing assumed.

  1. 1Send an enquiry

    One workflow, the people who handle it and what you need to understand.

  2. 2Focused walkthrough

    We show the relevant screens with sample records, and label what is sample.

  3. 3Written scope

    Available, configured and unfinished behaviour, in writing.

  4. 4Your decision

    Price and terms in writing before any paid engagement.

The first conversation

Tell us who will use it and what they do today.

Tell us who will use the product, what they do today and the workflow you want to explore. We can use those details to focus the evaluation.

Pricing

No public price is listed here. Ask for a written scope and price for your requirements before making a purchase decision.

What to ask for in the scope

  • The exact features and screens to be demonstrated.
  • A clear list of sample, configured and unfinished behaviour.
  • Any setup, hosting or external-provider requirements.
  • What support, data handling and responsibilities would apply.
  • Written costs and commercial terms before a paid engagement.
