Sound familiar?

The team discussed it. Someone agreed to do it. A week later, nobody can find the decision.

A client update sits in a chat. The action sits in a task list. The reason behind it stays in someone’s head. Finding the full picture takes another round of messages.

Keep the conversation, the decision and the follow-up within the same working environment.

What makes the approach worth exploring

Work with the context behind the task.

A task tells you what to do. A conversation explains how the team got there.

UniFlow brings those working records into the same environment: channels, reviewed decisions, ideas, tasks and goals. Evaluate whether that connection makes your own handovers easier to follow.

For business owners

Get back into the conversation without restarting it.

When several projects are moving at once, the hard part is often remembering what was agreed and what still needs an owner.

Use channels for working context, reviewed decisions for agreements, and tasks for follow-through.

For project leads

Make the handover easier to follow.

A new owner should not need five different people to explain the last decision.

Keep the project discussion and its follow-up records accessible in one workspace.
