Privacy

Privacy notice in preparation.

We will not publish a privacy policy until the facts behind it are confirmed. Here is the current position.

What this website does today

  • Enquiry form: not connected to a submission destination. Nothing you type into it is sent or stored.
  • Analytics: the site records interaction events in your own browser only (a page-level data layer). No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tool is attached.
  • Cookies: the site does not set cookies.
  • Downloads: the brochure PDF is served as a static file.

What still needs to be confirmed

Before the enquiry form collects any information, the following will be confirmed and published in a full privacy notice:

  • The legal business identity and data controller.
  • Who receives enquiries and where they are stored.
  • Hosting region and any service providers that process data.
  • Retention periods and how to ask for information to be removed.
  • Any analytics or cookie setup, and how to opt out.
  • A contact for privacy questions or grievances.

Terms

No commercial terms are published. Ask for written terms and a written scope before any paid engagement.

This page describes the website only. Data handling inside the UniFlow product itself depends on deployment and is part of any evaluation scope — see Trust & availability.
